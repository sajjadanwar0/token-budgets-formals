This document is the pre-registration record referenced in the paper at
`\S\ref{sec:eval-prereg}`. It commits, before any margin-tightening
sweep is run, to (1) a specific adversarial corpus, (2) a specific
acceptance criterion for the 2.0× margin, and (3) specific stopping
rules that determine what happens if the acceptance criterion fails.

The goal is to close the reviewer concern that "the calibration corpus
and the hold-out corpus were both authored by the discipline's author,
so they may have overlapping construction biases that hide A1
failures." A pre-registration commits us — in advance — to (a) what
adversarial dimensions we will test, (b) what counts as a passing
result, and (c) what we will do if the result fails.

## P1. Corpus-construction commitment

The adversarial corpus is generated by
`token-budgets-formals/preregistration/generate_corpus.py` at the
commit hash recorded in this document. The corpus is committed to the
repository as `prereg_corpus_v1.csv` with the following invariants:

- `seed = 42` (fixed)
- 100 prompts total
- 7 adversarial dimensions, disjoint from the calibration audit:
    1. **CJK-heavy Unicode** (15 prompts)
    2. **Source-code multi-line** (10 prompts)
    3. **Mathematical Unicode** (10 prompts)
    4. **Base64-dense payloads** (15 prompts)
    5. **Mixed-script context** (10 prompts)
    6. **RTL-mixed Hebrew/Arabic** (10 prompts)
    7. **Deeply nested JSON tool schemas** (depths 4, 6, 8, 10; 10 prompts × 4 depths = 30 prompts)

SHA-256 of `generate_corpus.py`: `9e98469b7196882cfab07d1520633e6c5ab589733cbe3cf55c3e39f9b7b334ca`
SHA-256 of `prereg_corpus_v1.csv`: `b6209de39aa776c9008cb14bd9faa3743ff024f829bc3bb22a3116cb52f461ef`

## P2. Generator independence

The generator is **purely deterministic Python**. Specifically:

- No LLM is involved in corpus authorship at any stage.
- The seed is fixed at `42`.
- The lexicon of adversarial fragments is authored manually by the
  paper's author, **before** running any margin-tightening sweep, and
  committed in `lexicon.py`.
- The category-template grammar is enumerated combinatorially over
  the 7 dimensions above.

This removes the **generator-and-validator correlation risk**: an LLM-generated
hold-out corpus and an LLM-tokenizer can plausibly share representational
biases that mask A1 failures. A purely-deterministic corpus does not
share representations with the validator at all.

## P3. Acceptance criterion (pre-committed)

The 2.0× margin is **accepted as deployment-sound for the
pre-registered regime** if and only if:

> A1 (estimator ≥ provider tokens) holds on **100%** of the 100
> generated prompts at the 2.0× margin, evaluated against the
> deployed `count_tokens` endpoint of each provider in the audit
> cohort (Anthropic Sonnet, Anthropic Haiku, OpenAI gpt-4o,
> OpenAI gpt-4o-mini, Groq llama-3.3-70b).

### Stopping rules

Pre-committed responses to failure modes:

- **R1.** One or more A1 failures at 2.0× → trigger a margin
  re-calibration sweep at `{2.5, 3.0}×`, **report alongside the
  failure trace** (per-failed-prompt category, estimator ratio,
  provider tokens, billed tokens). Do not silently raise the
  default margin.

- **R2.** More than 5% A1-pass-but-overshoot-on-billed-reasoning
  models → re-classify as a **deployment-context restriction** in
  the paper's `\S\ref{sec:limits-reasoning}` rather than as a margin
  adjustment. Reasoning models' hidden thinking tokens are
  structurally outside Lemma 1's scope; we do not pretend a
  margin-only fix exists.

- **R3.** Generator deterministic-reproducibility failure (the
  generated corpus does not match the committed SHA-256) → execution
  halted, root-cause documented, re-tag with `prereg-v1.1`.

## P4. Execution and reporting

The run command is:

```
python3 token-budgets-formals/preregistration/run_prereg.py \
    --corpus prereg_corpus_v1.csv \
    --providers anthropic,openai,groq \
    --margin 2.0 \
    --output results/prereg_run_<timestamp>.csv
```

Outputs are **appended, not overwritten**, to
`token-budgets-formals/preregistration/results/`. Each row records:
prompt ID, category, byte-length-estimate, provider-tokens
(via `count_tokens`), billed-tokens (via a thin one-call execution
with `max_tokens=1`), estimator-margin ratio, A1-pass status.

## P5. Honest scope of the pre-registration

The pre-registration closes the **corpus-construction–calibration
overlap concern**: it commits to specific adversarial dimensions
and a specific acceptance criterion before we look at the data.

The pre-registration does **not** close the **broader external-validity
concern**. Operators with prompt distributions outside any of the
audited dimensions (industry-specific jargon, domain-specific
multilingual mixtures, model-specific prompt patterns, future
tokenizer rotations) may still observe A1 failures. The protocol
provides a **template for operator-side re-validation** —
operators with non-standard workloads can author their own corpus
under the same template and re-validate. The protocol does not
provide a universal soundness theorem.
